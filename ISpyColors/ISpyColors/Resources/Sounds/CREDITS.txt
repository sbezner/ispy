Audio assets
============

win.m4a, fail.m4a
  Source: Kenney (kenney.nl) — "Music Jingles" and "Digital Audio" packs.
  License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal) — public domain,
           no attribution required. Credit given here voluntarily.
  Converted from .ogg to .m4a (AAC) for iOS playback.

The app also uses Apple's built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer (text-to-speech) to say
"I spy something <COLOR>!" — no audio assets are bundled for that.

To swap in your own sounds: drop a `win.m4a` / `fail.m4a` (or .caf/.wav/.mp3 —
update the extension in SoundEffects.swift) into this folder. The app prefers a
bundled file and falls back to a built-in system sound if none is found.
Good CC0 sources: kenney.nl/assets, mixkit.co/free-sound-effects, pixabay.com/sound-effects.
