What the thresholds mean / how to tune

These mirror the Swift defaults. Edit them at the top of the <script> to preview tuning: minMatchFraction=0.03, minBlobFraction=0.02, minChromaSaturation=0.20, minChromaValue=0.18. Orange & purple use a saturation floor of 0.20×0.7=0.14; pink uses 0.20×0.55=0.11. Changing them here does not change the app — port any winning values back into ColorDetector.swift.
